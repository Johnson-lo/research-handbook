## From local velocity to interval-average velocity

MeanFlow defines

$$
u(z_t,r,t)=\frac{1}{t-r}\int_r^t v(z_\tau,\tau)\,d\tau.
$$

Thus

$$
(t-r)u=\int_r^t v(z_\tau,\tau)\,d\tau,
$$

and the finite-time update is

$$
z_r=z_t-(t-r)u(z_t,r,t).
$$

For one-step generation:

$$
(r,t)=(0,1),\qquad z_0=z_1-u_\theta(z_1,0,1).
$$

## MeanFlow Identity

Differentiating the interval relation gives

$$
v=u+(t-r)\frac{du}{dt}.
$$

The total derivative is

$$
\frac{du}{dt}=\partial_z u\,v+\partial_tu=\operatorname{JVP}(u;v).
$$

### Mental model

Define accumulated displacement

$$A(r,t)=(t-r)u(r,t).$$

Then

$$\frac{dA}{dt}=v(t).$$

MeanFlow therefore uses a **local differential consistency condition** to learn a **long-range average transport field**.

## Original MeanFlow objective

Original MF constructs

$$
u_{tgt}=(e-x)-(t-r)\operatorname{JVP}(u_\theta;e-x),
$$

and trains

$$
\mathcal L_{MF}=\mathbb E\|u_\theta-\operatorname{sg}(u_{tgt})\|^2.
$$

The apparent target contains the current network through the JVP term, making it network-dependent.
