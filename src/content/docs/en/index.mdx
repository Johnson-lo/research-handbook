## Research Knowledge Graph

The homepage uses **Diffusion → Flow Matching → MeanFlow** as the method trunk, but not as a rigid tree. Select **MeanFlow** to expand representation, training dynamics, objective / JVP, endpoint quality, and distribution correctness; then select a branch to reveal connected papers, concepts, and transferable ideas from earlier methods.

## Paper Library

Each paper has an independent entry for its core question, derivations, method, training / inference, experiments, interpretation, and limitations. Cross-paper relationships are connected through the graph and Research Tracks.
