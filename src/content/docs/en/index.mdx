## Follow the method evolution from the underlying problem

The site organizes Flow Matching through Improved MeanFlow as one causal sequence:

**Why does Flow Matching usually need multiple NFEs?**
→ **Why does MeanFlow switch to average velocity?**
→ **How can an average velocity field be trained?**
→ **What limitations appear in the original MeanFlow objective?**
→ **What key pathway does Improved MeanFlow change?**
→ **What conclusions are supported by the experiments?**

### Site structure

1. **The Full Story**: the complete Flow Matching → MeanFlow → iMF narrative.
2. **Deep Dives**: MeanFlow Identity, JVP, and the iMF objective in more detail.
3. **Concept Reference**: distribution, conditional velocity, marginal velocity, and related foundations.
4. **Evidence & Sources**: paper claims and published experimental results.
