## What this site is

This is a **curated research handbook**, not a chronological blog. The goal is to turn paper reading, discussion, experiments, and research questions into a public knowledge base that can be revisited, extended, and used as a technical research portfolio.

### Three layers of content

| Layer | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Handbook** | Reconstruct the concepts in the order needed to understand the field. |
| **Paper Notes** | Preserve what each paper actually claims, derives, and reports. |
| **Interactive Lab** | Make distributions, velocity fields, JVP, and MeanFlow objectives physically inspectable. |
