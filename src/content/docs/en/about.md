This is a growing public research handbook focused on generative modeling, flow-based methods, and robotics.

The site uses three complementary layers:

- **Paper Library** for the problem, method, evidence, and interpretation of individual papers.
- **Research Tracks** for methodological evolution, branching directions, and open questions across papers.
- **Concepts** for reusable technical foundations and interactive explanations shared across multiple papers.

The primary language is Traditional Chinese, with technical terminology preserved in English for precision. A corresponding English version is maintained in parallel.
