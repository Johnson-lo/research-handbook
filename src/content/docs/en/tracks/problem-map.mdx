# Research Problem Map

The primary unit of this page is the **research question**, not the paper. A question can be partially answered by several papers and still leave a more specific experimental hypothesis unresolved.

<ResearchProblemMap lang="en" />

## Reading the graph

- **Foundation**: the framework on which the question depends.
- **Research Question**: a concrete issue surfaced through reading, derivation, implementation, or experiments.
- **Paper / Evidence**: theoretical, ablation, controlled-experiment, or empirical evidence relevant to the question.
- **Open hypothesis**: a more specific protocol that is plausible from current evidence but not directly established.

## Example evidence chain: should $r=t$ be learned first?

### Hypothesis

Because $u(z,t,t)=v(z,t)$ at the boundary, establishing boundary / instantaneous competence before large temporal gaps may be more stable than mixing all intervals from the start.

### Paper-supported evidence

**AlphaFlow** analyzes the heavy $r=t$ boundary supervision used by MeanFlow, decomposes the objective into trajectory flow matching and trajectory consistency, finds strong gradient conflict, and improves optimization by annealing from trajectory flow matching toward MeanFlow.

**Understanding, Accelerating, and Improving MeanFlow Training** uses controlled experiments to show that instantaneous velocity must form first, small-gap average velocity helps it, and large-gap supervision can damage it when introduced too early. The resulting curriculum moves from short to long temporal gaps.

### Still unresolved

A fixed-duration pure-$r=t$ pretraining stage is a more specific protocol than either paper directly establishes. It remains a clean ablation to test against small-gap warm-up and progressive-gap curricula.
