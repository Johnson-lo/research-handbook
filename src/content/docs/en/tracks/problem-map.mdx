# Research Knowledge Graph

This is not a one-way paper timeline and not a rigid tree in which every node has a single parent. **Diffusion → Flow Matching → MeanFlow** provides the mainline; MeanFlow then expands into research problems whose papers, concepts, and earlier-method design lenses can have multiple relationships.

## How to read it

- **Mainline**: the method lineage leading to MeanFlow.
- **Research branches**: representation / NFE, training dynamics, objective / JVP, endpoint / refinement, and distribution correctness.
- **Paper nodes**: work with direct theoretical or empirical evidence; select a paper to open its note.
- **Concept / transfer nodes**: design lenses from earlier methods. Dashed connections mean plausible transfer, not established effectiveness.

## A currently important cross-question

At $r=t$, $u(z_t,t,t)=v(z_t,t)$, directly linking boundary learning to instantaneous velocity. AlphaFlow and MeanFlow training-dynamics work also emphasize optimization conflict and temporal-gap ordering. This makes local/small-gap → large-gap learning a question that crosses training, representation, and objective stability rather than belonging to only one branch.
