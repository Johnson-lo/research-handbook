## 1. Original MF as an equivalent v-loss

From the MeanFlow Identity,

$$
v=u+(t-r)\frac{du}{dt},
$$

define

$$
V_\theta=u_\theta+(t-r)\operatorname{JVP}_{sg}(u_\theta;e-x).
$$

Original MF can be viewed equivalently as

$$
\mathcal L=\mathbb E\|V_\theta-(e-x)\|^2.
$$

The predictor can then be written as

$$
V_\theta(z_t,e-x),
$$

because the sample-specific $e-x$ enters the JVP tangent.

## 2. The iMF objective-level change

iMF uses

$$
V_\theta(z_t)=u_\theta(z_t)+(t-r)\operatorname{JVP}_{sg}(u_\theta;v_\theta).
$$

The supervision remains

$$
\mathcal L=\mathbb E\|V_\theta(z_t)-(e-x)\|^2.
$$

:::caution[Key invariant]
iMF does **not** remove $e-x$ supervision; it changes the tangent used in the JVP / predictor pathway.
:::

## 3. Where does $v_\theta$ come from?

Boundary condition:

$$
v(z_t,t)=u(z_t,t,t),
$$

so the same network can define

$$
v_\theta(z_t,t)\equiv u_\theta(z_t,t,t).
$$

The paper also studies a training-only auxiliary $v$-head.

## 4. Other iMF system changes

- random $\omega$ conditioning
- $\Omega=\{\omega,t_{min},t_{max}\}$ conditioning
- in-context conditioning
- removal of adaLN-zero
- SwiGLU / RMSNorm / RoPE
- longer training

The final FID 1.72 is therefore a system-level result rather than an objective-only result.
