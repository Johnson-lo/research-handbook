# Flow Matching → MeanFlow → Improved MeanFlow → RMFlow

This track follows one evolving question: **how can flow-based generation become much faster without introducing new training or distribution errors from aggressive one-step transport?**

<TrackMiniMap lang="en" />

## 1. Flow Matching: learn a local velocity

Flow Matching models $dz_t/dt=v_\theta(z_t,t)$. For the linear path $z_t=(1-t)x+te$, sample-level supervision is $e-x$, while the marginal MSE optimum is $v(z_t,t)=\mathbb E[e-x\mid z_t,t]$. Training is simulation-free, but inference still usually requires repeated ODE queries.

## 2. MeanFlow: change the modeled quantity

MeanFlow models interval-average velocity

$$
u(z_t,r,t)=\frac{1}{t-r}\int_r^t v(z_\tau,\tau)d\tau
$$

so that $z_r=z_t-(t-r)u(z_t,r,t)$. The new problem is that $u$ has no simple per-sample ground-truth label, motivating the MeanFlow Identity.

<DerivationStepper lang="en" />

See [Calculus & JVP](/research-handbook/en/concepts/calculus-jvp/) for a from-zero explanation of the derivatives.

## 3. Original MeanFlow: conditional tangent target construction

Original MF uses

$$
u_{tgt}=(e-x)-(t-r)\operatorname{JVP}(u_\theta;e-x),
$$

so $e-x$ appears both as supervision and as the JVP tangent.

## 4. Improved MeanFlow: a state-conditioned compound v-loss

<MFvsIMFBridge lang="en" />

The easy-to-confuse objects are lowercase $v_\theta$, the JVP, and capital $V_\theta$.

<IMFNotationExplorer lang="en" />

The core iMF predictor is

$$
V_\theta(z_t)=u_\theta+(t-r)\operatorname{JVP}_{sg}(u_\theta;v_\theta),
$$

with supervision $\mathbb E\|V_\theta(z_t)-(e-x)\|^2$. The key change is the tangent, not the supervision.

## 5. RMFlow: coarse transport plus refinement

[iMF](/research-handbook/en/papers/improved-meanflow/) mainly reformulates the objective and guidance. [RMFlow](/research-handbook/en/papers/rmflow/) follows a different branch: when coarse 1-NFE transport still leaves a distribution-quality gap, add a tailored noise-injection refinement after the single flow evaluation and couple it to a likelihood-related objective.
