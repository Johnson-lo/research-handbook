---
title: Interactive Lab
---

The lab turns equations into inspectable toy systems. The visualizations are explanatory, not experimental evidence.

- **Probability Path Explorer** — drag time and inspect how $z_t$ changes.
- **Conditional vs Marginal Velocity** — resample pairs and see why $e-x$ is not the unique field.
- **JVP Explorer** — inspect how interval length changes the meaning of average velocity.
- **MF vs iMF Objective Explorer** — switch among original MF, equivalent v-loss, and iMF.

The same components are embedded next to the relevant handbook equations so the visualization appears where it is conceptually useful.
